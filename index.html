This atlas follows the direct entries of Wikipedia’s Ferns of the Americas category. It is a snapshot of that category, not a complete taxonomic inventory of every American fern. Genera can be explored separately.

Species text is reproduced from Wikipedia and available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Every entry links to its article and revision history for attribution.

The botanical watercolors are AI-generated artistic interpretations. Reference images are linked where Wikipedia provides them. Illustrations are for discovery and appreciation, not definitive plant identification.

Move your pointer over a plate to rustle its fronds. Turn wind off in the navigation for stillness. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
